# Marvel Continuity Research (§51) — design anchors

Comic-first, MCU-second. Where continuity contradicts itself, we pick the interpretation that is
most **recognizable, comprehensive, and mechanically interesting** (§51), and note the choice.

## Dr. Doom
- **Full portfolio, not "armored MCU sorcerer":** genius technologist, sorcerer-supreme-tier
  mystic, powered armor, energy projection, force fields, robotics (Doombots), teleportation,
  strategy. The kit deliberately spans **tech + sorcery + melee + control** so no single facet
  dominates (§4). His personal **force field** and **Doombots** are near-constant across eras →
  first-class systems here, not flavor.
- **Character read (drives §42 animation):** monarch's composure, arrogance, minimal wasted
  motion. He assumes victory. No acrobatics.
- **Emperor Doom / God-Doom precedent:** stories where Doom holds cosmic-tier power (e.g.
  absorbing the Beyonder's power; *Secret Wars* God-Emperor) justify the **Emperor Doom**
  capstone as comic-anchored, not fanfic.

## Mjolnir (Marvel comic + Norse myth, distinguished, §14/§51)
- **Marvel additions vs myth:** the *worthiness enchantment* and Mjolnir being **uru** forged in
  a star are Marvel; classic Norse Mjöllnir is a dwarf-forged hammer that returns and makes
  lightning but has no "worthiness" clause and a famously short handle (a forging flaw). We keep
  both: uru + worthiness (Marvel) *and* the short-handle/returning identity (myth).
- **Worthiness handling (§15):** we expose **Canonical / Doom-Override / Unrestricted** modes so
  the mythic enchantment is respected *without* locking the player out (design agency). The
  Doom-Override leans on real precedent — Doom has schemed for/around Asgardian power more than
  once — framed as the player's custom continuity.
- **Powers:** flight, weather/storm control, the "return to hand," and lightning are all
  established → they are their own subsystems (throw/return is the signature, §17).

## Odinforce (Marvel)
- Marvel's **Odinforce/Odinpower** = vast All-Father energy: force projection, matter/energy
  manipulation, enchantment, dimensional feats, enormous augmentation. It is *authority*, not
  just a golden blast (§22). Hence the **matter/energy** and **divine-authority** ability
  groups (O9–O19), not only beams.
- Distinguished from Mjolnir's storm identity by **gold-white celestial** VFX and a
  transformation-state framing (Odin channels power through will).

## Infinity Stones (comic, beyond the films, §51)
- Comic Gems are **conceptual absolutes**, stronger and stranger than their MCU versions:
  - **Space** — total control of where things are (teleport, portals, spatial collapse).
  - **Mind** — access to and control of thought across beings (charm, dominion, psionics).
  - **Reality** — rewrite what is real/true (transform, invert rules, matter-strip).
  - **Power** — limitless energy that **amplifies the other Gems and any ability** (our PO7
    Amplify is the mechanical expression of this).
  - **Time** — full temporal control (slow/stop/rewind — bounded by engine, §A.4).
  - **Soul** — dominion over souls/life essence (drain, spectral, the Soul World motif).
- **Combined > sum:** the comics treat a full Gauntlet as reality-editing. Our Infinity State +
  "Infinity" ultimate + combined pairs (§34/§35) reflect *emergent* power, not six stacked buffs.
- **Boss-safe design is a lore-compatible compromise:** rather than trivially deleting named
  foes (which would end encounters), bosses take catastrophic-but-capped effects — preserving
  both the power fantasy and the game (§35/§45).

## Cross-system character rule (§36–39)
Even wielding Mjolnir, the Odinforce, or the Gauntlet, the player should still read as **Doom** —
channeling borrowed power through his own armor, sorcery, and strategy (posture, green-tech
framing on synergies, tech-assisted hammer throws). The point of difference from a "Thor build"
or a "Thanos build" is that Doom **appropriates** power; the animations and synergy abilities
encode that.

*Sources: general Marvel Comics continuity for Dr. Doom, Thor/Mjolnir, the Odinforce, and the
Infinity Gems; Norse mythology for Mjöllnir's forging and returning-hammer identity. Where eras
conflict, the most iconic/comprehensive reading was chosen per §51.*
